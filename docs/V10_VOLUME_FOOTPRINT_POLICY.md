# V10 Volume / Large-Participant Footprint Policy

BLACK ORACLE does not label an unexplained high-volume move as a specific actor, operator or manipulation event.

The runtime may only classify observable participation patterns such as:

- abnormal volume expansion versus recent baseline
- repeated volume expansion across bars
- high volume with limited downside progress (absorption-like)
- high volume with limited upside progress (distribution-like)
- close location within the bar
- price/volume divergence
- breakout or breakdown participation
- VWAP relation when available
- volume-profile location when available
- foreign/institutional/broker flow only when a separate verified dataset supplies it

Allowed outputs: `ACCUMULATION_LIKE`, `DISTRIBUTION_LIKE`, `MIXED`, `NONE`, `DATA_GAP`.

Specific actor identity remains null without independent source-backed flow/ownership data. These signals are evidence inputs, not execution authority.
