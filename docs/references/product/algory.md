## Why BLACK ORACLE references it

Algory is useful as a product precedent for making systematic strategy creation and automation legible to an end user. The reference is especially relevant to BLACK ORACLE's Strategy/Method lifecycle, testing surfaces, and the separation between strategy discovery and real execution.

## Patterns worth studying

- strategy campaigns / strategy generation as a visible product workflow;
- multiple strategies competing rather than one opaque "AI strategy";
- automation cards that expose strategy/timeframe state;
- simple strategy lifecycle visibility;
- presenting strategy diversity as a product feature;
- making testing and deployment feel like one coherent workflow.

## BLACK ORACLE translation

Use the *workflow principle*, not Algory's product identity:

- Strategy Factory / Method candidates should remain inspectable.
- Champion–Challenger status should be visible.
- Backtest / validation / Paper results should remain linked.
- Strategy collection performance should be separated from individual strategy performance.
- User-facing strategy controls must preserve BO evidence, risk, provenance, and authority semantics.

## Do not copy blindly

- performance claims or scoring methods;
- strategy-generation thresholds;
- deployment defaults;
- visual branding;
- any assumption that a generated strategy is execution-ready.
