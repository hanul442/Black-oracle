1. Market-state snapshots exist for all five horizons with explicit source timestamps.
2. Sector packets have sufficient constituent coverage and no hidden missing-data substitution.
3. KRX market-cap and crypto-exposure classification are operational and fail closed.
4. 1H/4H history is multi-session and reproducible.
5. Committee nomination and cross-review lineage is persisted.
6. Red Team and Head Council outputs are replayable.
7. Horizon Trade Maps include entry, invalidation, stop, targets and forecast range.
8. Shared-capital exposure is enforced across horizon sleeves.
9. Outcome attribution identifies horizon, strategy, signal families and committee contributors.
10. Paper evidence passes a separate promotion review; no automatic live authority.
